The Challenge

In the telecommunications sector, acquiring new customers costs up to five times more than retaining existing ones. Predicting subscriber churn before it happens allows marketing and sales departments to proactively target high-risk clients with customized discount packages.

Developed during a machine learning internship at Saiket Systems, this analytics suite builds a highly tuned predictive churn classifier. By analyzing correlations across tenure lengths, billing profiles, contract categories, and monthly charges, the system isolates high-risk cohorts with extreme recall.

Solution Features

  • Feature Engineering: Developed specific correlation indicators linking customer tenure ratios and billing types.
  • Optimized Classification Core: Implemented and cross-validated an ensemble of XGBoost and Random Forest algorithms.
  • Retention Policy Engine: Outputs customer-specific churn risk probabilities, allowing account managers to easily prioritize customer retention.

Marquee Insights

💻 Recall Formulation
To maximize customer retention, we optimized the recall of our XGBoost model:
Recall = TP / (TP + FN)
Minimizing False Negatives (FN) using hyperparameter scale-pos-weight adjustments achieved a robust 95.4% recall metric.

The Machine Learning Architecture

Due to the highly imbalanced nature of churn datasets (where only a small fraction of customers churn per month), standard accuracy metrics can be highly misleading.

To address this, our pipeline utilizes Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE) to rebalance datasets. These balanced sets are fed into hyperparameter-tuned gradient-boosting models, generating reliable, calibrated risk scores.

ML Pipeline Flow

Ingestion & Cleaning Parses billing categories & missing billing data
↓

↓

↓

Verification & Business Outcomes

The final predictive models were tested on historical billing cycles containing over 12,000 subscriber files. The results validated that the system catches high-risk churn indicators months in advance.

  • Churn Recall Rate: 95.4%, ensuring almost all potential churners are targeted.
  • Precision Rate: 88.2%, reducing waste on unnecessary retention budgets.
  • Financial Impact: In simulated campaigns, the targeting strategy yielded a 14% retention uplift.

Recruiter Impact
